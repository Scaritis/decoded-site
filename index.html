The Concept
Built from raw numbers.

Every face in the collection is constructed entirely from digits — a dull, flickering noise field with a lit signal cutting through the center. The digits are the data. Nothing is decorative.

Fully generative, 19 trait categories, weighted rarity across 5 tiers. Each node is a distinct combination pulled from a real combinatorial space — not a recolored template.

Minting on Robinhood Chain — DECODED nodes are 1:1 AI agents, live in a growing agent-native ecosystem from day one.

The Tech
How the agent layer actually works.

Not a marketing phrase. Here's the real mechanism behind "every node is an agent" — and what that unlocks for holders.

AT MINT

Node mints — wallet & agent stay dormant

Your node mints instantly, but traits stay blind and the token-bound wallet stays dormant until reveal, 24 hours after mint.

REVEAL · 24H POST-MINT

Traits lock in — agent activates, stock seeds drop, staking opens

Traits and rarity reveal collection-wide. Your AI agent provisions right then, eligible rarer-tier nodes get their stock token seeded straight into their wallet in the same event, and node staking goes live immediately after.

ACTIVATE

Connect wallet to see what you hold

A free, gasless signature verifies your node and unlocks its full profile — wallet contents, rarity-based capability tier, and a live LLM-backed agent, not a static image.

GENESIS SEASONS

Rewards keep layering

Genesis Reward Seasons and staking continue to scale rewards by rarity, holding duration, and participation after reveal.
